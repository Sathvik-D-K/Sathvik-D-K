🔬 Professional Summary & Value Proposition
As an AI Engineer with a Master of Science in Mechatronics and over 2.5 years of dedicated industrial experience, I specialize in architecting, optimizing, and deploying complex Computer Vision and Deep Learning pipelines. My work is heavily focused on transitioning theoretical neural network architectures into highly efficient, scalable production environments while managing the end-to-end machine learning lifecycle.

🌍 Location & Work Status: Based in Jena, Germany. Holder of German Permanent Residence (Unrestricted Work Permit).

🚀 Production Impact & Optimization: Engineered and deployed automated ML/CV inspection models achieving 95%+ accuracy in semiconductor defect classification. Formulated and deployed algorithmic defect pre-filtering pipelines that reduced computational overhead and processing time by 90%.

🧠 Research & Academic Publications: Authored a peer-reviewed publication in Springer Nature (2024) focusing on One-Shot Defect Fingerprint Comparability using Siamese Neural Networks to classify ultra-rare defect classes in highly data-scarce environments.

⚙️ Current Engineering Focus: Designing agentic workflows with Generative AI (RAG, LLMs, LangChain), implementing the Model Context Protocol (MCP) for secure tool integration, and fortifying MLOps pipelines (Docker, MLflow, AWS) to ensure complete model lifecycle reproducibility and monitoring.
